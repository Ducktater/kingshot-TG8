The long winding road to TG8

About this tool

This tool estimates how long it takes to reach TG 8 starting from your current state, while also bringing Barracks, Range and Stable up to their maximum tiers along the way.

It prioritises spending TG, TTG and speed-ups during KvK prep week to earn as many points as possible. Multi-day upgrades (Town Center and Embassy) are deliberately delayed so their build time can be burned down with speed-ups during prep week — each day of speed-up spent earns points. Barracks, Range and Stable are only started when enough speed-ups are banked to finish them instantly, so they never have to stop printing troops.

The plan skips Strongest Governor and other events — it only models the KvK prep-week schedule.

TG income from events is not counted. Only the Daily Income field drives TG income. Any extra income will have to be added to the balance manually.

Disclaimer: the further into the future the plan projects, the less accurate it becomes. Re-run the calculation periodically with your current balances and building state for the best estimate.
